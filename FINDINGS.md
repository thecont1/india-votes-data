# What 70,186 Trips Reveal About Bangalore Traffic

> **Dataset:** 70,186 trips across 13 routes  
> **Period:** September 2025 – June 2026 (8.5 months)

---

## 1. The Worst Trip in Bangalore History

**107 minutes for 10.6 km**

On **June 2, 2026 at 7:06 PM**, someone on the **East Outer Ring** (Benniganahalli Metro Station → Embassy TechVillage) crawled at **5.9 km/h**.

> *That's walking speed. For almost two hours.*

The same evening, **Old Airport Road** also hit **8.6 km/h**. June 2nd was catastrophic across the board.

---

## 2. Old Airport Road: The Most Unpredictable Route

With a **coefficient of variation of 34.7%**, Old Airport Road is the route where you have the *least* idea how long your trip will take.

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Best trip | **43.8 km/h** |
| Worst trip | **8.4 km/h** |
| Ratio | **5.2x** |
| Best time | ~13 minutes |
| Worst time | ~71 minutes |

> You could get there in 13 minutes or in 71. Good luck.

---

## 3. East Outer Ring: Bangalore's Road of Extremes

This route holds the **all-time worst trip record** (5.9 km/h) but its volatility is staggering:

| Time / Day | Speed |
|------------|-------|
| 2 AM average | **39 km/h** |
| 7 PM average | **22 km/h** |
| Tuesday (worst day) | **25.8 km/h** |
| Sunday (best day) | **34.4 km/h** |
| Best vs Worst ratio | **7.8x** |

That's an **8.6 km/h spread** just from day-of-week alone.

---

## 4. Sarjapur Road: The One Good News Story

This is the **only route that genuinely improved**:

```
Oct 2025:  22.3 km/h
Mar 2026:  25.7 km/h  (+15% improvement)
Jun 2026:  22.9 km/h  (June knocked it back)
```

Still better than where it started — but fragile.

---

## 5. Double Decker Flyover: The Consolation Prize

It looks fast at **28.7 km/h** average, but there's a catch:

| Period | Average Speed |
|--------|---------------|
| Oct – Dec 2025 | **29.0 km/h** |
| Apr – Jun 2026 | **28.1 km/h** |

It's the **only central route trending downward**.

> *"Things have gotten worse — traffic is heavier."*

---

## 6. Central Diagonal 2: Bangalore's Slowest Route, Period

At **20.7 km/h** average, this is the slowest corridor in the city.

```
17.7% of ALL trips are below 15 km/h  →  nearly 1 in 5 trips
```

For comparison, **Airport Expressway** never once dropped below 15 km/h in **5,457 readings**. Not once.

---

## 7. The Evening Curse Is Real (And Getting Worse)

Every single route except East Outer Ring is **worse in the evening rush (5–7 PM)** than the morning rush (8–10 AM).

| Route | Evening Penalty |
|-------|-----------------|
| Airport Expressway | **-8.9 km/h** (evening is 22% slower) |
| Sarjapur Road | **-3.7 km/h** |
| East Inner Ring | **-3.5 km/h** |
| Old Airport Road | **-3.2 km/h** |

The evening rush compresses speeds to a brutal **15–17 km/h** across most city routes.

---

## 8. The Dead Zone: When You're Basically Walking

| Route | % of trips below 15 km/h |
|-------|--------------------------|
| Central Diagonal 2 | **~18%** |
| North Outer Ring | **~12%** |
| Old Airport Road | **~10%** |

> The city's "average" speed masks how often traffic is literally **slower than cycling**.

---

## 9. Saturday Is the Worst Day (Not Friday)

Across almost every route, **Saturday is the slowest day**:

| Route | Saturday Speed |
|-------|----------------|
| East Outer Ring | **26.3 km/h** |
| Central Diagonal 2 | **20.1 km/h** |
| Mysore Road | **23.6 km/h** |
| South Outer Ring | **20.9 km/h** |

**Sunday** is consistently the fastest. The weekend "improvement" everyone assumes? **It only happens on Sunday.** Saturday is just as bad as a weekday — sometimes worse.

---

## 10. The Airport Runway: 1.7x Faster Than Everything Else

| Metric | Airport Expressway | City Average | Gap |
|--------|-------------------:|-------------:|-----:|
| Average speed | **42.2 km/h** | **25.1 km/h** | **+17 km/h** |
| 2–3 AM peak | **56 km/h** | — | — |
| Same hour, Central Diagonal 2 | — | **30 km/h** | **-26 km/h** |

> The airport road exists in a **different Bangalore entirely**.

---

## The Big Picture

Bangalore traffic hasn't dramatically worsened over 8 months — the city-wide average barely budged from **26.3 → 25.6 km/h**. But it hasn't improved either.

**The real story is the extreme variance.**

> The difference between a good trip and a nightmare trip on the same route can be **4–8x**.

Planning a journey in Bangalore isn't about knowing the average — it's about knowing **which 20% of trips will ruin your evening**.
